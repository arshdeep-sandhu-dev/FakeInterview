# SUP-104: Stock stuck in "reserved" and never comes back

**Reported by:** Merchandising
**Priority:** P2

---

After Black Friday, the inventory dashboard shows **~200-300 units across nearly every SKU**
sitting in `reserved`. They never return to `available`. There are no pending orders that could
explain them. Products look "low stock" or "sold out" on the site when the warehouse shelves are
full.

What we noticed:
- The reserved count jumped around **23:58**, when the payment processor had a ~10 second
  brown-out.
- It kept creeping up **after the limited sneaker drop sold out (~23:55)**, even on SKUs that were
  nowhere near selling out.

**Current workaround:** someone on the ops team runs a script every morning that sets `reserved`
back to 0 for every SKU. Please make that unnecessary.
