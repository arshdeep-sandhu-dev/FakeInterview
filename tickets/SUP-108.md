# SUP-108: Fully refunded orders stuck as PARTIALLY_REFUNDED

**Reported by:** Customer Support
**Priority:** P3. Annoying, and it blocks us from closing tickets.

---

When we click **"Refund in full"** in the admin tool, the refund goes through for the full amount
and the customer gets all their money back. But on **some** orders the status becomes
`PARTIALLY_REFUNDED` instead of `REFUNDED`. The admin tool then shows:

```
ord-1001 status=PARTIALLY_REFUNDED total=$25.50 refunded=$25.50 refundable=$0.00
```

The order can't be closed because it isn't "fully refunded," but there's nothing left to refund.

Most orders work fine. We looked for a pattern by customer, product, region and date and didn't
find one. A support agent thinks it might happen "more on round-number orders," but isn't sure.
