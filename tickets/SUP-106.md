# SUP-106: Order page shows a different total than the receipt, and "Refund in full" fails

**Reported by:** Customer Support
**Priority:** P1. We can't process returns for Black Friday orders.

---

Customer `cus-0412` says their order page shows **$155.86** but their receipt email and bank
statement both say **$123.61**. Since Monday we've had dozens of these. All of them were placed on
**Friday night before midnight**.

When we click **"Refund in full"** in the admin tool on these orders, the payment processor rejects
it:

> HTTP 502: Payment processor rejected the refund: Refund of $140.72 would exceed captured amount
> $106.97 for txn-700059 (already refunded $0.00)

Finance also says that when they **re-ran Friday's daily sales report on Monday**, several category
totals (Electronics, Home, Toys) were higher than when they ran it Saturday morning. Grocery was
unchanged. No new Friday orders were added in between.

Orders placed after midnight seem fine.
