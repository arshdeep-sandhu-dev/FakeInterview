# SUP-107: No confirmation email, and "Something went wrong" after being charged

**Reported by:** Customer Support
**Priority:** P1

---

A few percent of Black Friday orders **never got a confirmation email**. Many of the same customers
say checkout showed:

> Something went wrong. Please try again.

**But their card was charged**, and the order shows as PAID in the admin tool. Some of them tried
again and now have **two orders**. A few also say they **didn't get loyalty points** for the order.

It seems to affect our **B2B / business accounts** more than regular shoppers, but not all of
them, and not every order they place.

The error tracker shows a spike of HTTP 500s on `/checkout` that night. Nobody has had time to look
at it yet.
