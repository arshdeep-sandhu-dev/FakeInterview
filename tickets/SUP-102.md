# SUP-102: Customers charged twice for a single order

**Reported by:** Payments team (escalated from Support)
**Priority:** P1. We have 12 chargeback threats so far.

---

Since Black Friday night, customers keep writing in with screenshots of **two identical charges** on
their card for one order. For every case we've checked:

- Our order database has **one** order, status PAID, with one transaction id.
- The processor's dashboard shows **two captures** of the same amount, a few hundred milliseconds
  apart. **Both reference the same ShipFast order id.**
- It lines up with the times the processor was slow. Their status page shows elevated latency and
  timeouts that night.

We asked the processor. They say their API "behaved as documented" and pointed us at their
idempotency docs. Nobody on our side has read those docs recently.

Example: order `ord-1043` shows captures `txn-700211` and `txn-700214`, both $123.61, 0.4s apart.

We can't reproduce it by placing orders by hand in staging.
