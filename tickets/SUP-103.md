# SUP-103: We oversold the limited "Midnight" runner

**Reported by:** Warehouse Operations
**Priority:** P1. This was the most hyped drop of the year.

---

We received exactly **40 pairs** of `AP-SNKR-LTD` (Limited Runner "Midnight"). The drop went live
at 23:55 on Black Friday and sold out within seconds.

**43 customers** got a confirmation and were charged. We had to cancel 3 of them. Social media is
not happy.

The stock ledger for the SKU doesn't add up:

| SKU         | received | available | reserved | sold | available + reserved + sold |
|-------------|---------:|----------:|---------:|-----:|----------------------------:|
| AP-SNKR-LTD | 40       | 0         | 0        | 43   | **43**                      |

A handful of ordinary SKUs are also off by 1 to 3 units in **both** directions. Some show more than
we received and some show less.

QA clicked through the drop in staging with two browsers and couldn't make it oversell.
